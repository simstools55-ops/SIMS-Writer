# Publication Finalization Gate v3.0.1

The gate evaluates the final repaired candidate, not the initial draft.

- `PUBLIC_OK`: publishable now.
- `PUBLIC_OK_WITH_USER_DECISION`: publishable changes plus genuine user-owned choices. Decisions declare `blocking: true|false`.
- `PUBLIC_BLOCKED`: unresolved SEO-critical issue after repair.

Quality recommendations are retained internally or shown as optional advice. They do not change the publication state.
