# Progressive Editing

SEO editing should progress at the component level rather than stopping or approving an entire article as one unit.

- Full SERP verification permits full planning subject to evidence and QA.
- Partial SERP verification permits supported presentation-layer edits while reserving uncertain structural or content expansion for human decision.
- Unavailable SERP verification permits only non-SERP mechanical and authoritative factual corrections.
- Evidence strength and verified scope apply separately to title, meta, introduction, headings, FAQ, body, images and links.
- A blocked component must not suppress an unrelated safe correction. A safe component must not authorize an unsupported claim elsewhere.
- The user receives finished edits and actionable decisions, not internal scores or gate traces.
