# Publication Pipeline Integrity

A prose PASS declaration is not evidence of QA execution. A valid final package must expose initial verdict, final verdict, review trace, unresolved findings, and publication status.

The release gate must detect:
- winner-query removal caused by mechanical title shortening;
- unsupported causal or universal numeric claims;
- unverified SERP-cause attribution;
- empty strings and unchanged entries in `changes[]`;
- internal-link decision/status mismatches;
- missing Validation or Publication QA evidence.
