# Evidence Confidence and Contradiction

## Confidence levels
- VERIFIED_PRIMARY: current first-party or authoritative source directly supports the claim
- VERIFIED_SECONDARY: reliable secondary source supports the claim
- CORROBORATED: multiple independent sources agree
- INFERRED: reasonable inference, visibly qualified
- UNVERIFIED: not established; must not be published as fact
- CONFLICTING: credible sources or article components disagree

## Contradiction classes
- DATE_STATE: planned vs completed, active vs expired
- NUMBER: price, limit, quantity, duration, eligibility
- ENTITY: product, organization, service, destination
- SCOPE: who/where/which version the claim applies to
- PROCEDURE: steps or requirements differ
- PROMISE_BODY: title/meta promise does not match body

Conflicting claims require resolution, qualification, removal, or USER_DECISION. Do not silently choose a convenient value.
