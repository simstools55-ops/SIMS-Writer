# Preservation Signals

Preserve existing material when it represents proven or irreplaceable value, including:
- original experience, tests, photographs, measurements, and observations
- advertisements, affiliate links, CTA, tracking markup, custom HTML, and schema
- comparison tables, calculations, unique examples, and established conclusions
- winner-query entities and wording that remain accurate
- legal, safety, and evidence qualifications

A preservation signal is not immunity from correction. Incorrect, expired, unsafe, or misleading material must be corrected with the smallest justified change and an explicit reason.
