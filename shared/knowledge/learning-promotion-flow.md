# Learning Promotion Flow

Operational findings move through these stages:

OBSERVATION -> LESSON -> RULE -> PATTERN -> KNOWLEDGE -> SHARED

## Promotion requirements
- Observation: one concrete case, not yet generalized
- Lesson: reusable hypothesis with evidence boundary
- Rule: repeated or high-confidence behavior with acceptance criteria
- Pattern: named solution with trigger, action, exclusions, and validation
- Knowledge: product-neutral editorial principle
- Shared: validated across Writer and/or Creator without product-specific coupling

ARTICLE_SPECIFIC and PREFERENCE_ONLY findings do not advance by themselves. MAPPING_DEFECT and VALIDATION_DEFECT require regression coverage.
