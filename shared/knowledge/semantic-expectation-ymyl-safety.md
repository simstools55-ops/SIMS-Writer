# Semantic, Expectation and YMYL Publication Safety

## Publication priority
Safety and factual accuracy override SERP opportunity and CTR optimization.

## Semantic title rule
Do not combine a numeric limit with an action that changes a different concept.

- NG: `写真1000枚を増やす`
- OK: `上限は1000枚`
- OK: `容量不足を解決する`

## Expectation rule
Every promise in article title, SEO title and meta description must be fulfilled in the article. Claims such as `賃貸OK`, `体験談`, or `3選` are blocking when the corresponding content is absent.

## YMYL safety rule
Safety information is not selected by search demand. When omission could increase harm, include a concise, evidence-aligned warning even when Search Console demand is absent.

## Benefit claim rule
Words such as `十分`, `改善`, `効果`, and named health outcomes must identify scope and evidence. Unsupported benefit claims must be softened, moved to user decision, or rejected internally.

## Whole-article safety alignment

タイトル・メタ・導入だけを安全化しても、本文、見出し、FAQ、チェックリストに危険な因果関係や保証表現が残る場合は不合格とする。局所免責は記事全体の意味構造を上書きしない。
