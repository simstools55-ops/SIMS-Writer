# Temporal Lifecycle Recovery Pattern

## Trigger
The article's current tense, promise, instructions, or deadline no longer matches the evidenced lifecycle phase.

## Action
1. Confirm the current phase with authoritative evidence.
2. Record the transition from old phase to new phase.
3. Identify stale facts, promises, CTAs, FAQ answers, and future-tense placeholders.
4. Preserve still-valid context and winner-query entities.
5. Update title/meta/introduction only when their promise is stale.
6. Replace speculative or pending sections with verified current procedures.
7. Validate deadline, date, number, entity, and scope consistency.

## Exclusions
Do not rewrite evergreen sections merely because a date changed. Do not convert uncertain status into a definitive claim.
