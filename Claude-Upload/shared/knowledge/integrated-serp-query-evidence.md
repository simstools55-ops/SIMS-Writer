# Integrated SERP / Query / Evidence Principle

Search Console queries show observed demand, verified SERP results show the current interpretation of intent, and reliable evidence determines what may be stated. None of the three should be treated as a substitute for the others.

- Query + SERP + sufficient evidence: eligible for publication planning.
- Query/SERP relevance + incomplete evidence: USER_DECISION.
- Plausible claim without evidence: INTERNAL_REJECT.
- A low-evidence claim must not leak into another PUBLIC_OK component.
